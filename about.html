OUR MISSION

Small Changes.
Meaningful Impact.

Food waste is a daily challenge that affects households and the environment. Many food items are forgotten or expire before they can be used.

ECHO aims to make food management easier by helping users organize their pantry, monitor expiration dates, and make better decisions about their food.
